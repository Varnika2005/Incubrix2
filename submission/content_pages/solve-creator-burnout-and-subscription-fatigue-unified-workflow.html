Diagnosing "The Second Job" in Your Creator Business

As the IncuBrix Manifesto (`https://incubrix.com/manifesto`) states: > *"Creating and distributing are different jobs. Only one of them deserves you. Being seen shouldn't require six subscriptions."* When auditing a typical creator's monthly software stack, six separate tools often appear: 1. Niche keyword/trend researcher ($30–$50/mo) 2. Transcript video editor ($24–$35/mo) 3. AI short-clip extractor ($19–$29/mo) 4. Animated caption generator ($20/mo) 5. AI voiceover & avatar tool ($22–$59/mo) 6. Multi-channel social scheduler ($15–$99/mo) Beyond costing $130–$290/month, every tool boundary forces manual file exports, gigabytes of local storage transfers, and repetitive formatting.

The 90-Minute Weekly Batch Production Protocol

1. 15 Minutes — Topic Selection (`Content Insights`): Pick one high-gap topic from your niche shortlist. 2. 45 Minutes — Keystone Recording: Record one 45-to-60-minute deep dive video or podcast episode. 3. 20 Minutes — Transcript Cut & 12-Clip Extraction (`/studio` & `/repurpose`): Delete false starts in the transcript, approve the 12 extracted highlight clips, and generate LinkedIn/newsletter text variants. 4. 10 Minutes — Single-Screen Distribution (`/publish`): Push sized and captioned outputs to YouTube, Instagram, LinkedIn, Spotify, and Bluesky.
