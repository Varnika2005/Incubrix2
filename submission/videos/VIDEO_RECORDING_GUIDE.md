# Video Demonstration Recording & Submission Guide

According to the official **IncuBrix 2027 Graduate Hiring Assessment Brief (Item 5)**:
Submit two unedited video recordings:
1. **(a) Product Walkthrough (Max 5 minutes):** Present product observations, target creator problems, the 4 IncuBrix superpowers, and the 90-day launch kit.
2. **(b) Technical Demonstration (Max 8 minutes):** Demonstrate single-command launch, one normal run, test suite execution, refresh on new input, and recovery from a failure.

---

## 1. Scripts Prepared for Recording

- **Product Walkthrough Script:** [`submission/videos/PRODUCT_WALKTHROUGH_SCRIPT.md`](./PRODUCT_WALKTHROUGH_SCRIPT.md) (4 min 45 sec word-for-word script).
- **Technical Demo Script:** [`submission/videos/TECHNICAL_DEMO_SCRIPT.md`](./TECHNICAL_DEMO_SCRIPT.md) (7 min 30 sec step-by-step terminal & UI script).

---

## 2. Recording Tool Recommendations (Free & Unedited)

1. **Loom (Free):** Screen + webcam overlay, instant link generation.
2. **OBS Studio (Free & Open Source):** Record 1080p MP4 screen recording with microphone audio.
3. **Microsoft Clipchamp / Xbox Game Bar (Windows Built-in):** Press `Win + G` to capture screen and microphone.

---

## 3. How to Link Your Videos in the Final Submission Workbook

Once uploaded (to Google Drive, Loom, YouTube Unlisted, or Dropbox):
1. Open `submission/workbook/IncuBrix_2027_Growth_Engineer_Option_3_AEO_SEO_GEO_Submission_Workbook.xlsx`.
2. Go to tab **`00_START`**:
   - Paste Product Walkthrough link in cell **`B14`** (`Product walkthrough video URL (max 5 min)`).
   - Paste Technical Demo link in cell **`B15`** (`Technical demo video URL (max 8 min)`).
3. Save the workbook.
