Build the operating system the creator economy runs on.

Millions of people record something great, then lose their week to editing, captioning, and re-uploading. IncuBrix ends that, and it is live. Come build what creators open every morning.

Our purpose is simple to say and hard to build: empower every creator to transform ideas into impact and passion into lasting success. We intend to shape the creator economy for the next 50 years. That work is underway, with a small team and a live platform. Early people shape the build.

The culture that gets us there · tap a value

Eight things we refuse to compromise.

01Nº 01Creator-ObsessedNorth Star+

Creators are our North Star. Every decision starts with one question: does this give a creator more reach or more time?

02Nº 02Think 10x, Execute 100xAmbition+

Incremental thinking builds incremental products. We aim for order-of-magnitude ideas, then out-execute everyone on the details.

03Nº 03Extreme OwnershipBehaviour+

Every team member is a founder here. You own outcomes, not tickets. If something is broken, it is yours to fix.

04Nº 04Fast, Without Compromising QualityCraft+

Speed is a habit, not an excuse. We ship weekly and we ship things we are proud of.

05Nº 05Transparency, Zero PoliticsTrust+

Information flows freely. Decisions are argued in the open, on merit. The best idea wins regardless of whose it is.

06Nº 06Win as One TeamTogether+

No silos, no lone heroes. Product, engineering, and success move as one unit toward one scoreboard.

07Nº 07Do More With LessLean+

Constraints sharpen us. A lean team with clear priorities beats a big team with meetings.

08Nº 08Integrity, AlwaysFoundation+

Ethical AI, fair monetization, real security. We are building trust with creators for decades, and we will not trade it for a quarter.
