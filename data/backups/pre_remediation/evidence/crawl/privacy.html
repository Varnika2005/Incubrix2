Legal

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 24 March 2026

1. Scope and Applicability

This Privacy Policy applies to:

  • All users of the IncuBrix Platform worldwide
  • Access via web applications, mobile applications, APIs, integrations, and enterprise deployments
  • Free, paid, trial, and enterprise users

This Policy does not apply to third-party websites or services linked from the Platform.

2. Our Role

IncuBrix acts as a data controller for personal data relating to account creation, billing, support, platform administration, security, analytics, and marketing.

Where IncuBrix processes customer content or personal data on behalf of a business customer under its instructions, IncuBrix acts as a data processor or service provider, as applicable.

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information (name, email address, company name, contact details)
  • Profile information and user preferences
  • Content you create, upload, submit, or process using the Platform
  • Payment and billing information
  • Communications with IncuBrix (support requests, emails, feedback)

3.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device and technical information (IP address, browser type, operating system)
  • Usage data (pages visited, features used, timestamps, session duration)
  • Log files, diagnostic data, and error reports
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies

3.3 Information from Third Parties

  • Authentication providers (e.g., SSO services)
  • Payment processors (transaction confirmations only)
  • Analytics and infrastructure providers

4. How We Use Your Information

User data is used solely to provide, operate, and improve core platform functionality. Specifically, we use personal information to:

  • Provide, operate, maintain, and improve the Platform
  • Create and manage user accounts and subscriptions
  • Process payments and confirm transactions
  • Send administrative notices, updates, and security alerts
  • Respond to inquiries and provide customer support
  • Monitor usage, analyze trends, and improve performance
  • Develop new features, products, and services
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, abuse, or security issues
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our agreements

5. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR and UK GDPR)

If you are located in the EU, EEA, or UK, we process personal data based on one or more of the following lawful bases:

    • Contractual Necessity: to provide the Services you request
    • Consent: where you have provided explicit permission
    • Legitimate Interests: to operate, secure, and improve our Platform
    • Legal Obligations: to comply with applicable laws

You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting prior lawful processing.

6. AI Features and Data Processing

6.1 AI Training and Improvement

IncuBrix may use aggregated and de-identified data to improve its Services, analytics, reliability, and AI-related functionality. IncuBrix does not claim ownership of user inputs or outputs. Where user content is used for model or product improvement, IncuBrix will describe that use in the applicable product experience, contract, or settings. Enterprise customers may be offered additional controls under separate agreements.

6.2 AI-Generated Content

AI-generated outputs may not be unique, and similar content may be generated for other users. IncuBrix does not claim ownership of AI-generated content created by users.

6.3 Third-Party AI Models

Certain AI features rely on third-party AI model providers. When you submit prompts, inputs, or files, such data may be processed by these providers solely to generate outputs.

Important: Unless expressly permitted by a separate agreement or feature-specific notice, please do not submit sensitive personal data through AI prompts, uploads, or workflows, including government identifiers, financial account details, health information, biometric data, or data about children. You are responsible for ensuring that any personal data you submit is appropriate for your use of the Services and lawful to process.

7. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising purposes.

We may share data only in the following circumstances:

  • 7.1 Service Providers

    Trusted third-party service providers may support hosting, infrastructure, analytics, authentication, communications, payment processing, customer support, and AI-related functionality. These providers may process data on our behalf under contractual obligations designed to protect it.

  • 7.2 Business Transfers

    In the event of a merger, acquisition, restructuring, or sale of assets, personal data may be transferred subject to appropriate safeguards.

  • 7.3 Legal and Safety Requirements

    We may disclose information where required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of IncuBrix, our users, or the public.

8. Payments

Payments are processed by third-party payment processors. IncuBrix does not store full credit card numbers or sensitive payment credentials. We retain limited transaction records for accounting and compliance purposes.

9. Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain business and security records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements.

If you delete your account, we will delete or de-identify your personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required for legal, security, fraud prevention, accounting, or backup purposes. Some data may remain in secure backups for a limited additional period before deletion.

10. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Request deletion of personal data
  • Restrict or object to processing
  • Receive a portable copy of your data
  • Opt out of marketing communications

Requests can be made by contacting support@incubrix.com. We may verify your identity before responding and will comply within legally required timelines.

11. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enable essential platform functionality, remember preferences, analyze usage, and provide personalized experiences (where permitted). You may control cookies through your browser settings, though disabling them may affect Platform functionality.

12. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including encryption in transit and at rest, access controls, and regular security reviews. However, no system is completely secure. You are responsible for safeguarding your account credentials. If a data breach affecting personal data occurs, we will investigate, take appropriate remediation steps, and provide notifications to affected individuals and relevant authorities where required by applicable law.

13. International Data Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries outside your country of residence. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

14. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not intended for individuals under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If such data is identified, it will be deleted promptly.

15. Jurisdiction-Specific Rights

15.1 Singapore (PDPA) If you are a resident of Singapore, you have additional rights under the Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), including the right to access and correct your personal data and to withdraw consent, subject to applicable legal exceptions.

15.2 European Union (GDPR) If you are located in the EU or EEA, you have rights under the GDPR, including the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, or object to processing, the right to data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

16. Data Processing Addendum (DPA)

For enterprise customers where IncuBrix acts as a data processor, a Data Processing Addendum will be provided upon request to ensure compliance with GDPR, PDPA, and other applicable regulations.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on the Platform with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the Platform constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.

18. Connected Accounts, Integrations and Publishing Data

When you choose to connect third-party accounts, social media accounts, publishing channels, business accounts, creator accounts, or other external services to the IncuBrix Platform, we may collect and process information made available through those integrations.

This may include account identifiers, profile information, usernames, page or channel identifiers, business account information, access permissions, authentication tokens, media files, captions, post content, publishing status, scheduling information, post identifiers, content metadata, engagement or performance data, and related technical information.

We use this information only to provide, operate, secure, and improve the IncuBrix Platform, including to help users connect accounts, create content, manage content workflows, schedule or publish content, display publishing status, retrieve content or performance information where enabled, troubleshoot issues, and maintain account security.

We do not sell personal data or connected account data. We do not use connected account data to build unrelated user profiles or for advertising purposes. We share such data only with trusted service providers where necessary to operate the Platform, where directed by the user, or where required by law.

19. Data Deletion Requests

Users may request deletion of their IncuBrix account data, personal data, content, and connected account or integration data at any time by contacting support@incubrix.com.

Users may also disconnect integrated accounts from the IncuBrix Platform where this option is available. When an integrated account is disconnected, IncuBrix will stop using that integration for future account access, publishing, scheduling, or data retrieval.

Upon receiving a valid deletion request, IncuBrix will verify the request, delete or de-identify the relevant personal data and connected account data within a reasonable period, and confirm completion where appropriate.

Deletion may include account profile data, connected account identifiers, authentication tokens, publishing metadata, scheduled content, uploaded content, generated captions, integration records, and related technical data, unless retention is required for legal, security, fraud prevention, accounting, backup, dispute resolution, or compliance purposes.

Some deleted data may remain in secure backups for a limited period before being permanently removed according to our backup retention schedule.

20. YouTube API Services and YouTube Data

This Section applies only to IncuBrix features that access or use YouTube API Services. If this Section conflicts with another provision of this Privacy Policy, this Section controls only for YouTube API Data.

  • 20.1 Use of YouTube API Services

    IncuBrix uses YouTube API Services to provide YouTube-connected publishing, channel-management and analytics features.

    Use of IncuBrix's YouTube-connected features is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service at https://www.youtube.com/t/terms.

    Google and YouTube process information in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

  • 20.2 YouTube Information Accessed

    Depending on the features and permissions authorized by the user, IncuBrix may access, collect, store and process:

    • Google and YouTube account and channel identifiers;
    • channel name, profile information and channel metadata;
    • OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, authorized scopes and connection status;
    • authorized video identifiers, titles, descriptions, tags, thumbnails, captions, publishing status and visibility settings;
    • channel, video and performance information returned through YouTube API Services, including authorized analytics, reporting and statistics;
    • Content and metadata the user instructs IncuBrix to upload, publish, schedule, update or manage; and
    • technical, security and audit records relating to the YouTube connection and requested actions.

    IncuBrix does not request or store the user's Google or YouTube password.

  • 20.3 Use and Sharing of YouTube API Data

    IncuBrix uses YouTube API Data to provide the user-facing YouTube features authorized by the user, including:

    • connecting and identifying the user's YouTube channel;
    • displaying authorized channel and Content information;
    • uploading, publishing, scheduling or managing Content at the user's direction;
    • displaying publishing status;
    • retrieving authorized analytics and performance information;
    • generating user-requested exports;
    • maintaining security;
    • troubleshooting errors; and
    • complying with applicable law and YouTube requirements.

    YouTube API Data may be shared:

    • with Google and YouTube to authenticate the user or perform an action requested by the user;
    • with service providers processing data on IncuBrix's behalf under contractual confidentiality and data-protection obligations;
    • with agents expressly authorized by the user; and
    • where required by law or necessary for security or abuse prevention.

    IncuBrix does not sell YouTube API Data or make identifiable Authorized YouTube Data available to other users except agents expressly authorized by the authorizing user.

    IncuBrix will not use YouTube API Data to replace YouTube-supplied metrics or create new or derived YouTube metrics unless expressly permitted by YouTube.

  • 20.4 Cookies and Similar Technologies

    IncuBrix and its service providers may store, access or recognize cookies, local storage, device information and similar technologies for authentication, security, session management, preferences and operation of connected features.

    Google or YouTube may also use cookies or similar technologies when a user completes Google authorization, loads a YouTube feature or interacts with YouTube Content. Google's processing of such information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

  • 20.5 Storage and Refresh

    Authorization tokens may be retained only while reasonably necessary to provide the features authorized by an active user and may be used only for purposes consistent with that authorization.

    Authorized YouTube analytics, reporting information and API statistics may be retained as permitted by YouTube, provided IncuBrix verifies at least every 30 calendar days that the user's authorization remains valid.

    Other Authorized YouTube API Data and limited Non-Authorized YouTube API Data will be refreshed or deleted within 30 calendar days.

    IncuBrix will use reasonable efforts to display the most current YouTube API Data available. Historical information will be identified with its relevant date or period.

  • 20.6 Revocation and Deletion

    Users may revoke IncuBrix's access using the "Disconnect YouTube" control in the Platform or through Google's security settings at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.

    When a user disconnects YouTube through IncuBrix, requests deletion of stored YouTube data or deletes their IncuBrix account, IncuBrix will revoke the applicable authorization token and delete the corresponding Authorized YouTube API Data as soon as possible and within seven calendar days.

    Where access is revoked directly through Google's settings, IncuBrix will delete the associated YouTube API Data as soon as the revocation is detected and no later than 30 calendar days following revocation.

    Deleting information stored by IncuBrix does not delete information held by YouTube. To delete information held by YouTube, the user must use YouTube or another authorized application that supports that action.

  • 20.7 Contact

    Questions, complaints, revocation requests and deletion requests concerning YouTube API Data may be sent to:

    Email: support@incubrix.com
